## Continuity Lock

- **Product:** Nord Atelier Soren Weekender 40L in matte full-grain Warm Saddle Tan vegetable-tanned leather (`#8B5A2B`) with hand-burnished dark-espresso edges (`#3A2618`), 4 mm waxed-linen saddle stitching, rolled leather top handles, dark-charcoal waxed-canvas shoulder strap, and solid satin-brushed antique brass (`#B69659`) square roller clasps, side D-rings, and swivel snaps. Subtle blind-debossed uppercase `NORD ATELIER` mark sits centered 24 mm below the front handle gusset (`S01` front angle only; cleanly absent on unmarked side/rear views in `S02` and `S03`).
- **Lighting and palette:** Fixed low-angle ~4600K directional Nordic morning sunlight from **screen left** streaming through tall iron-and-glass clerestory windows, balanced by cool ~6200K indirect limestone bounce fill on screen right. Disciplined 35mm Kodak Vision3 250D palette: Warm Saddle Tan (`#8B5A2B`), Deep Espresso (`#3A2618`), Brushed Brass (`#B69659`), Limestone Chalk (`#E8E3DA`), Morning Slate (`#5C6773`), Charcoal Wool (`#2B2E31`). Zero studio sweep gloss or crushed blacks.
- **Space, cast, and axis:** Single continuous Scandinavian limestone-and-glass railway concourse with pale honed stone benches along the left edge and a vaulted travertine colonnade leading straight back toward tall arched glass windows. Single understated traveler (never shown full-face) in matte charcoal wool overcoat and heather-grey knit cuff; right hand closes the front clasp (`S01`), both hands lift handles onto right shoulder (`S02`), bag rides on right hip walking away into concourse depth (`S03`). Camera stays strictly on the south-facing side of the **180-degree axis** so screen-left sunlight and left-to-right / foreground-to-background progression hold across every cut.
