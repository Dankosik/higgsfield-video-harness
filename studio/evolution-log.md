# Studio Evolution Log (Self-Improvement Memory)

Meta-memory (SIM) tracking *why* harness rules, validators, and skills were added or changed,
what failure trace triggered them, and when to retire or revisit them as models improve.

### EV-01 (2026-11-20) — Interactive R0–R5 Dailies Loop & Mechanical Lock Guards
- **Trigger trace:** One-shot autonomous generation hid taste misalignment until after credits were burned (`git:509f49e`, `git:92eb285`).
- **Change:** Pivoted to interactive `R0`–`R5` Director ↔ Crew loop with `taste-log.md`, `LOCKED` immutability enforced in `scripts/hf_guard.py`, dual budget walls (`Campaign cap` + `--round-cap`), and minimalist `AGENTS.md` kernel.
- **Expected effect:** Zero unapproved credit spend or accidental overwrites of Director-locked assets.
- **Observed effect:** Verified mechanically in `tests/test_hf_guard.py` and `campaigns/example-product-spot/`.
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Never retire budget/lock walls; simplify round ceremony only for single-shot non-campaign tasks.

### EV-02 (2026-11-20) — Native 1080p Upscale Gate, 1:1 Crop QA & Batch Cut Audit
- **Trigger trace:** Shrunk `480px` `3x2` contact sheets hid bright edge halos, muddy faces, `1918x1080` rounding, and `1.4x–2.0x` Laplacian HF spikes on `480p -> 1080p` generative upscales (`git:77c9fd7`).
- **Change:** Added `--crops` unscaled `1:1` inspection and `batch --strict` to `scripts/qa_media.py`, `480p` pixel-upscale warning to `scripts/check-campaign.py`, and native `1080p` Upscale Gate to `higgsfield-cli`.
- **Expected effect:** Zero upscaler shimmer or resolution/FPS mismatch reaching Dailies 5.
- **Observed effect:** Covered by automated regression tests in `tests/test_qa_and_assemble.py` and `tests/test_check_campaign.py`.
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Generative video upscalers pass blind `1:1` `--crops` and `hf_sharpness_outlier <= 1.3x` checks across `>= 2` production campaigns.

### EV-03 (2026-11-20) — Typed R2B Asset Bible, Spatial Lock & Expression Calibration
- **Trigger trace:** Disconnected reverse-angle generation caused 180° room flips, wardrobe drift, and mid-clip facial morphing on multi-angle spots (`git:cc0f72e`, `git:2b29959`, `git:7d7a976`).
- **Change:** Added typed `R2B` Asset Bible (Reverse Walls, neutral `#808080` prop plates, Expression Calibration), `qa_media.py slice`, and ref-budget/CEL guards in `scripts/check-campaign.py`.
- **Expected effect:** Eliminate 180° room flips, scale drift, and mid-clip face collapse on multi-angle spots.
- **Observed effect:** Validated via `tests/test_check_campaign.py` and `tests/test_qa_and_assemble.py`.
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Native 3D-consistent video generators hold 4-wall geography and reverse angles without explicit Reverse Wall plates across `>= 2` multi-angle campaigns.

### EV-05 (2026-11-21) — Narrative Architecture Skill, Narrative Lock & Blind Viewer Read QC
- **Trigger trace:** Multi-round production campaigns exposed meaning leaks surviving green pixel/continuity QA: cold viewers misreading product category (mistaking a visual metaphor for hardware), zero-delta filler shots cut only after `R4B` motion spend, ambiguous hero-object silhouettes failing `0.1s` recognition on mute, and synthetic prop groupings lacking human truth (`git:46c57f2`).
- **Change:** Added canonical `.agents/skills/video-narrative/` (`Narrative Lock`, `BUT / THEREFORE` causal spine, per-shot `Narrative job` + Deletion Test, `0.1s` instant-read semiotics, `2 + 2` subtext, and runtime-scaled commercial/non-commercial structures), wired `R3`/`R4B` Blind Viewer Read + `NARRATIVE_DEFECT` into `video-qa`, `qa_media.py`, `check-memory.py`, `delegation.md`, and `reviewer-agent`, and added backwards-compatible `narrative_lock` / `narrative_job` checks to `check-campaign.py` plus `State shift` / `Primary misread risk` prompts in `campaign_init.py`.
- **Expected effect:** Catch category misreads, ambiguous `0.1s` objects, and zero-delta filler beats at `0 cr.` (`R1B`/`R3`) before motion spend while keeping creative taste strictly with the Director.
- **Observed effect:** Verified mechanically via `make check`, `tests/test_repo_checks.py`, `tests/test_check_campaign.py`, and `tests/test_memory_and_retro.py`.
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Revisit after `>= 3` multi-shot campaigns; graduate `narrative_lock` or `narrative_job` from soft-optional to warning-level in `check-campaign.py` only if agents routinely omit them.

### EV-06 (2026-11-22) — Multi-Flow Auto-Router, Composable Sub-Flows, Strict MP4/Temporal QA & Partial-Take Assembly
- **Trigger trace:** Live multi-shot commercial campaign audits plus synthesis of 28 Higgsfield production case studies exposed five structural gaps: self-graded `PASS` reviews logged with `qa_report: null`, 6-frame sheets missing temporal stutters/bitrate jumps, `assemble.sh` lacking trims/grades forcing ad-hoc `xfade` scripts, delayed real-photo grounding, and single-track workflow coverage.
- **Change:** Added 6-track `docs/flows.md` auto-router with sticky `production_track` lock + composable `sub_flows` (`remotion_overlays`, `vfx_hero_shot`, `v2v_rescue_or_recam`, `shorts_cutdown`) + 5 modular flow skills (`video-v2v-rewrite`, `video-previz-vfx`, `performance-ads`, `hybrid-motion-graphics`, `youtube-clipping`); upgraded `scripts/assemble.sh` (`shots[].edit` trims/4K punch-in/pre-LUT grade/fade-out, `--duck-music`, `--overlay`, `--bpm`), `scripts/qa_media.py` (full-FPS stutter/jerk telemetry, cross-cut luma jump & bitrate disparity, `pixel-diff`, fail-closed `R4/R5 PASS` proof gate), `scripts/check-campaign.py` (`outfit` kind, `production_track`, `sub_flows`, prompt timing, receipt drift, `R0` real-photo grounding), and upgraded `reviewer-agent.toml` + core skills.
- **Expected effect:** Zero rubber-stamped `PASS` reviews, automated detection of micro-stutters and cross-cut quality jumps, surgical partial-take harvesting without ad-hoc FFmpeg hacks, and clean automatic routing across 6 production modes plus plug-in sub-flows.
- **Observed effect:** Verified offline via expanded `make check` (`test_qa_and_assemble.py`, `test_check_campaign.py`, `test_memory_and_retro.py`, `test_repo_checks.py`, ShellCheck, docs/instructions/memory lint) plus live end-to-end campaign verification, which drove six immediate refinements: auto-sliced `<= 20s` master review chunks (`qa_media.py render --verify-slices-dir`) and bounded reviewer turn budgets, VO cadence/pause telemetry (`speech_start`, `internal_pauses`) + `trim_start_s`/`trim_end_s` track trimming, fail-closed `--sheet` requirement on `R4/R5 PASS`, `kling3_0` photocopy-cascade check, mandatory reading of declared `sub_flows[]` skills, and `media/work/` temp overlay hygiene.
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Revisit individual flow skills or merge overlapping tracks if future Higgsfield native tools collapse V2V, 3D previz, and motion-graphics separation into a single deterministic studio interface.

### EV-07 (2026-11-22) — Viewer-Quality Fail Bar, Shot-Density Gate, Lip-Sync Lock & Verbatim Short-Cue Captions
- **Trigger trace:** Blind end-to-end Shorts verification passed every technical metric (`-14 LUFS`, `0` stutters, `24fps`, `9/9` logged reviews) yet failed real human viewing for four root reasons: (1) stretching `5` pre-existing stills into `11–15s` single-shot I2V clips caused paw/prop/background morphing and severe pacing fatigue, (2) silent `--sound off` I2V on visible talking characters produced out-of-sync random mouth flapping and wrong-speaker jaw movement, (3) `5–9s` paraphrased multi-line subtitle walls contradicted spoken audio (including an unfixed mispronounced word), and (4) reading `4` MP4 slices in one reviewer turn triggered context compaction and redundant re-reads.
- **Change:** Added `> 8s` single-shot I2V drift warning + `trim_out`-aware prompt timing check to `scripts/check-campaign.py`; added hard `FAIL` rows to `video-qa/SKILL.md` and `reviewer-agent.toml` for visible out-of-sync lip flapping or accidental non-speaking mouth motion, caption walls (`> 7` words / `> 3.5s`) or audio-caption mismatch, static `> 8s` pacing fatigue, and hand/prop/seam artifacts; enforced `<= 2` `.mp4` reads per reviewer turn (`delegation.md`, `reviewer-agent.toml`); added Shot-Density Gate (`2.5–5.5s` avg cuts; expand sparse stills before `R4`) to `docs/workflow.md` and `video-narrative/SKILL.md`; added verbatim `2–6` word (`1.0–2.5s`) caption pacing law to `hybrid-motion-graphics/SKILL.md`; and updated `video-direction`, `model-selection.md`, `characters-and-expressions.md`, and `[ENG-11]` to distinguish visible on-camera lip-sync (`intone`/Lipsync Studio/native speech) from naturally silent characters under off-screen VO (where silent I2V with closed-mouth direction stays fully supported).
- **Expected effect:** Bridge the gap between "green technical metrics" and genuinely watchable human-grade pacing, lip-sync accuracy, crisp captions, and clean character anatomy while cutting master-review time below `2.5 min`.
- **Observed effect:** Verified mechanically via `make check` (`tests/test_check_campaign.py`, `check-instructions`, `docs-check`, `memory-lint`).
- **Retire / Revisit when:** Revisit the `8s` single-shot I2V warning threshold only if future video models hold stable hands, props, and dynamic camera choreography natively past `12s` across `>= 2` campaigns.
